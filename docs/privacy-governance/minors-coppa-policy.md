# CompassU Minors & COPPA Operating Policy

**Status:** Production operating draft for counsel review

## Intended audience

CompassU is designed primarily for high-school juniors, high-school seniors and college freshmen. It is not designed primarily as a service directed to children under 13.

## Under-13 rule

CompassU should not knowingly permit independent under-13 account creation/use unless a legally appropriate authorization path has been established for that deployment.

For a school-sponsored deployment involving children under 13, CompassU may rely on school authorization only when legally appropriate and only for the school's authorized educational purpose. Information collected under school authorization must not be used for unrelated commercial purposes.

Where school authorization is relied upon, CompassU will provide the school with appropriate notice describing the information collected and how it is used/disclosed, and will provide a process through which the school can request review, deletion, or cessation of further use/collection where required.

## Technical execution

Schools do not receive direct database access or unrestricted deletion controls. Authorized review/export/deletion actions are executed by CompassU Master Administrators after verification of the requester's identity, authority and student/institution scope.

## Data minimization

For under-13 users, CompassU will collect only information reasonably necessary for the authorized educational service and its security/operation. Age-verification information, if introduced, must be limited to age-determination purposes, protected, and retained no longer than reasonably necessary for that purpose unless law requires otherwise.

## Commercial restrictions

CompassU will not sell children's personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build unrelated commercial profiles from information collected through school-sponsored educational use.

## Retention

Children's personal information will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, authorized educational requirements, security/audit needs, or legal obligations, then securely deleted or de-identified according to the CompassU retention standard.

## Parent/guardian requests

Where applicable, CompassU will provide a reasonable process for verified parent/guardian review and deletion rights. For institution-controlled records, CompassU may coordinate with the institution to authenticate and fulfill the request. Technical execution remains with CompassU Master Administrators.

## Product requirement before under-13 deployment

Before intentionally onboarding an under-13 cohort, CompassU must complete a deployment-specific COPPA review covering audience/age handling, notice, consent/authorization path, collection minimization, parent/school review and deletion process, service providers, retention, and UI language. A general pilot intended for older students should not silently expand to under-13 users without this review.
