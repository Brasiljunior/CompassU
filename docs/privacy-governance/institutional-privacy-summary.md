# CompassU Institutional Privacy & Data Governance Summary

**Status:** Procurement-facing draft for legal review

CompassU is an educational career and college discovery platform designed primarily for high-school juniors/seniors and college freshmen.

## What student information is used for

CompassU uses student information to administer accounts, deliver and score assessments, generate educational major/career recommendations, provide college/career exploration, deliver requested reports, provide authorized institutional analytics, support users, secure the platform and maintain/improve the educational service.

## Core commitments

- CompassU does not sell student personal information.
- CompassU does not use student personal information for targeted advertising.
- CompassU does not use institution-provided student information for unrelated commercial profiling.
- Standard institutional analytics are aggregate by default.
- Small analytical cells below five students are suppressed where applicable.
- Identifiable administrative operations are restricted to authorized CompassU Master Administrators.
- Institutions do not receive direct database access or unrestricted deletion controls.
- Institutions can submit authenticated requests concerning records under their authority; CompassU validates and executes approved requests through its Master Administrator process.
- CompassU maintains data-retention, security, incident-response and subprocessor documentation.

## FERPA approach

When an institution relies on FERPA's school-official framework, the institutional agreement/DPA is designed to establish the authorized institutional function, direct-control terms governing CompassU's use and maintenance of education records, purpose limitations and restrictions on redisclosure.

## Children under 13

CompassU is not primarily directed to children under 13. An intentional under-13 school deployment requires a deployment-specific COPPA review and appropriate authorization/notice process. Information collected through school authorization must remain limited to the educational context and cannot be repurposed for unrelated commercial use.

## Security

CompassU's security program includes protected administrator access/MFA, database authorization controls, privileged-function protections, audit logging, security headers, secrets management, vulnerability management, backup/recovery practices, and monitoring/incident-response controls as implemented and tested through the production-readiness program.

## Service providers

Production infrastructure currently includes Supabase for backend/database/authentication services, Vercel for application hosting/delivery, and Resend for transactional email. CompassU maintains a production subprocessor disclosure and updates it as the architecture changes.

## Requests and deletion

Institutions may submit verified requests for records within their authorized scope. Students/parents/eligible students may submit requests as applicable. CompassU may coordinate with the institution to authenticate education-record requests. Technical access/export/correction/deletion execution remains exclusively with authorized CompassU Master Administrators.
