# CompassU Privacy Notice

**Status:** Production draft for legal review  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

CompassU provides educational career and college discovery services to students and participating educational institutions. This Privacy Notice explains the information CompassU processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and how authorized privacy requests are handled.

## Information CompassU processes

Depending on how CompassU is used, information may include account identifiers and contact information; first and last name; institution affiliation; graduation year and state when provided; authentication and account activity; assessment answers, progress, attempts and completion status; scores and derived recommendations such as major matches, career alignment and explanations; saved/favorite majors; institutional membership and role information; invitation and email-delivery information; administrator audit records; and technical/security information reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service.

CompassU also maintains reference information about majors, occupations, career clusters, institutions and programs. Such reference information is generally not student personal information.

## How information is used

CompassU uses information to create and administer accounts; deliver and autosave assessments; calculate and explain educational recommendations; provide career, major and college exploration; generate and email requested reports; provide authorized institutional analytics; support users and institutions; maintain security and prevent misuse; troubleshoot and improve the reliability and educational functionality of CompassU; satisfy legal obligations; and enforce applicable agreements.

CompassU does not sell student personal information. CompassU does not use student personal information for targeted advertising. CompassU does not use institution-provided student information to create unrelated commercial profiles.

## Educational institutions and student records

When CompassU processes student information for a participating institution, CompassU processes that information for the authorized educational service described in the institution's agreement. Where applicable, the parties may structure the relationship so CompassU performs an outsourced institutional service under FERPA's school-official framework.

Participating institutions do not receive direct database access or unrestricted administrative deletion authority. Technical execution of identifiable data access, export, correction and deletion operations is restricted to authorized CompassU Master Administrators. Institutions may submit authenticated requests concerning records under their authority, and CompassU will validate and execute approved requests through its Master Administrator process.

## Institutional analytics

CompassU may provide participating institutions with authorized analytics concerning participation, assessment completion, educational interests and recommendation trends. Standard institutional analytics are designed to be aggregate by default. CompassU applies institution scoping and privacy safeguards, including suppression of small analytical cells below five students where applicable. Identifiable operational records are provided only when authorized and appropriate for the service.

## Service providers

CompassU uses service providers to host and deliver the application, database/authentication services, email delivery and other necessary infrastructure. Service providers are authorized to process information only for contracted purposes and are expected to protect information appropriately. CompassU maintains a current subprocessor disclosure for production services.

## Security

CompassU uses administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and service. Safeguards include access controls, protected administrative functions, administrator multi-factor authentication, audit logging, transport encryption, database authorization controls, vulnerability management and production security monitoring. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention and deletion

CompassU retains information only for periods reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet authorized institutional requirements, maintain security/audit evidence, resolve disputes and satisfy legal obligations. CompassU maintains a written retention and deletion standard. When an authorized deletion request is approved, deletion is executed by an authorized CompassU Master Administrator according to that standard, subject to legal holds, security records and backup lifecycle limitations.

## Children and minors

CompassU is designed primarily for high-school juniors, high-school seniors and college freshmen and is not directed primarily to children under 13. CompassU does not knowingly permit independent under-13 use unless an appropriate school-sponsored or parent-authorized process has been established. Where COPPA applies, CompassU follows applicable notice, consent, review, deletion, data-minimization, security and retention requirements.

## Privacy requests

Students, parents/guardians where applicable, eligible students, and participating institutions may submit privacy requests through the contact method designated by CompassU. CompassU may require identity, authority and institutional verification before acting. Where a request concerns institution-controlled education records, CompassU may coordinate with the applicable institution before taking action. Approved technical actions are executed by authorized CompassU Master Administrators.

## Changes to this Notice

CompassU may update this Notice as the service, law or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date and, when appropriate, additional notice.
