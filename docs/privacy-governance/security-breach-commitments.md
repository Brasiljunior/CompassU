# CompassU Security & Breach Commitments

**Status:** Production draft; align with Item #6 incident-response implementation before final publication

## Security program

CompassU will maintain reasonable safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity and volume of information processed. The program should include:

- role- and scope-based access control;
- restricted privileged operations;
- multi-factor authentication for CompassU administrators;
- secure authentication/session handling;
- encryption in transit and provider-supported encryption at rest;
- database authorization/RLS and protected privileged functions;
- audit logging for material administrative operations;
- dependency/vulnerability management;
- security headers and appropriate cross-origin controls;
- secrets management without client exposure of privileged keys;
- backups and recovery procedures;
- monitoring and alerting;
- documented incident response and escalation;
- periodic security review/testing.

## Incident classification

CompassU will distinguish ordinary operational errors from suspected security incidents and confirmed breaches. A confirmed breach is an incident involving unauthorized acquisition, access, use or disclosure of protected information for which notice is required by applicable law or contract.

## Institutional notification

For a confirmed incident affecting an institution's covered information, CompassU will notify the affected institution without unreasonable delay after confirmation and provide reasonably available information about the nature of the incident, affected information/population, containment/remediation measures and recommended institutional actions. Contract/state-specific deadlines may supersede this baseline.

CompassU should not promise a fixed number of hours in public materials until Item #6 establishes an operational response capability capable of meeting that promise.

## Cooperation

CompassU will reasonably cooperate with affected institutions in investigating and responding to confirmed incidents, including preserving relevant evidence, supporting legally required notices, and documenting remediation, subject to security/confidentiality constraints.

## Subprocessors

CompassU will use reasonable diligence in selecting production subprocessors that handle protected information and will maintain contractual/data-protection arrangements appropriate to each provider's role.

## Continuous improvement

Material incidents, recovery tests, vulnerability findings and significant architecture changes should result in documented review and, where appropriate, corrective actions.
