# CompassU Institutional Data Processing & FERPA Addendum

**Status:** Production contract draft for counsel review

This Addendum applies when CompassU processes student or institutional personal information on behalf of a participating educational institution (Institution).

## 1. Purpose and roles

CompassU processes covered information to provide the contracted educational assessment, recommendation, reporting, support, security and related services. The Institution determines the authorized educational use of institution-provided education records, subject to the agreement and applicable law. CompassU determines the technical means reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service while remaining bound by the permitted-use restrictions in this Addendum.

Where the Institution relies on FERPA's school-official exception, the parties intend CompassU to perform an institutional service or function for which the Institution would otherwise use employees or other school officials; to be under the Institution's direct control with respect to the authorized use and maintenance of education records through this Addendum and the governing agreement; to use personally identifiable information only for the purposes for which it was disclosed; and to comply with applicable restrictions on redisclosure.

## 2. Permitted processing

CompassU may process covered information only as reasonably necessary to provide, secure, support, maintain and improve the contracted educational service; generate authorized reports and analytics; respond to authorized support/privacy requests; prevent fraud, abuse or security incidents; comply with law; and enforce the governing agreement.

CompassU will not sell student personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use institution-provided student information to build unrelated commercial profiles.

Product improvement using institution-provided student information must remain related to operation, security, reliability or educational functionality of CompassU and must not convert student information into an unrelated commercial asset.

## 3. Data categories

Covered information may include student names/contact identifiers, institution affiliation, profile information, authentication/account metadata, assessment responses and progress, derived scores/recommendations/explanations, saved preferences, reporting information, and operational/security records associated with the service.

## 4. Administrative control and requests

CompassU retains exclusive technical administrative authority over database-level access, identifiable exports, account modifications and deletion execution. Institutions do not receive direct database access or unrestricted administrative deletion privileges.

The Institution retains the right to submit authenticated instructions concerning records within its authorized scope. CompassU will reasonably verify the requester's identity, authority and scope before execution. Approved technical actions will be executed by an authorized CompassU Master Administrator and, where appropriate, recorded in an audit trail.

Nothing in this administrative model is intended to prevent an Institution from exercising rights or responsibilities applicable to education records under its control.

## 5. Access and correction

CompassU will reasonably assist the Institution in responding to lawful requests to inspect, access or correct covered education records maintained through CompassU. When appropriate, CompassU may provide records to the Institution so the Institution can authenticate and respond to the student, parent or eligible student.

## 6. Security

CompassU will maintain reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of covered information, including authentication/access controls, administrator MFA, authorization controls, encryption in transit, audit logging, vulnerability management, protected privileged operations, backup/recovery practices and incident response procedures.

## 7. Subprocessors

CompassU may use subprocessors necessary to operate the service. CompassU will maintain a current production subprocessor list and will require subprocessors that process covered information to protect it consistently with their contracted role and applicable requirements.

## 8. Security incidents

CompassU will maintain an incident-response process. If CompassU confirms unauthorized acquisition, access, use or disclosure of covered information that requires notice under the governing agreement or applicable law, CompassU will notify the Institution without unreasonable delay and provide information reasonably available to support the Institution's response obligations. Institution/state-specific agreements may establish a shorter fixed notification period.

## 9. Retention and disposition

CompassU will retain covered information only as reasonably necessary for the service, authorized institutional requirements, security/audit purposes and legal obligations. Following contract termination or a validated deletion instruction, CompassU will delete or de-identify covered information according to the CompassU retention/deletion standard unless retention is required by law, legal hold, security/audit necessity or an agreed institutional requirement. Backup copies will age out according to the applicable backup lifecycle and will remain protected while retained.

## 10. Aggregated and de-identified information

CompassU may produce aggregate/de-identified institutional analytics related to the educational service. Standard institutional reports should exclude direct identifiers and apply privacy controls including small-cell suppression where appropriate. CompassU will not attempt to re-identify properly de-identified information except for legitimate security/privacy validation.

## 11. Minors/COPPA

If an Institution authorizes use by children under 13 and relies on school authorization permitted by COPPA, CompassU will limit covered children's information to the authorized educational context and will not use such information for unrelated commercial purposes. CompassU will provide the Institution information reasonably necessary regarding collection/use/disclosure practices and a process for authorized review, deletion and prevention of further collection/use as required. Technical execution remains with CompassU Master Administrators.

## 12. Audit/cooperation

CompassU will reasonably cooperate with institutional privacy/security reviews, subject to confidentiality, security and proportionality limitations. CompassU may satisfy recurring diligence requests through current security/privacy documentation rather than exposing sensitive system details.

## 13. Order of precedence

If this Addendum conflicts with the governing institutional agreement regarding covered information, the more specific data-protection term controls unless the parties expressly agree otherwise.

## 14. Counsel completion items

Before signature, counsel should complete legal entity names, governing law, defined statutory terms, state student-privacy requirements, breach-notice deadlines, insurance/security schedule if required, audit language, international-data terms if relevant, and signature blocks.
